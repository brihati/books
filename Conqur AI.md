# Conqur AI

Conqur AI is an AI platform for Shopify merchants and growth teams. Merchants or agencies can describe, in plain English, the changes they want to make to a Shopify store and get those changes implemented safely.

The current product is focused on Shopify storefront edits. This includes small theme changes, section updates, layout fixes, campaign updates, mobile improvements, and other store changes that merchants usually wait on freelancers, agencies, or developers to complete.

Conqur AI already includes the foundation for a holistic Shopify execution platform: Shopify theme versioning, duplicate theme workflows, safe previews, and automated publishing. The goal is to make store changes faster, safer, and easier for non-technical teams.

## Current Focus

Our immediate focus is small Shopify store edits.

This is the clearest wedge because many Shopify merchants regularly need small changes but do not have a full-time Shopify developer. Their alternatives are usually slow, expensive, or risky: waiting for a freelancer, asking an agency for every change, editing theme code manually, or using drag-and-drop tools that may not match the store properly.

The first ideal customers are Shopify merchants and growth teams that regularly need storefront changes shipped quickly. This can show up as frequent campaigns, offers, product drops, landing pages, bundle tests, mobile fixes, CRO improvements, or basic store maintenance. We should not restrict the initial market by category too early, because different Shopify categories have different change cadences and different reasons to need storefront execution.

## Product Roadmap

1. **Store edits:** Plain-English requests for Shopify theme and storefront changes, with versioning, previews, and safe publishing.
2. **Campaign and page builder segment:** Move into the PageFly/Replo/GemPages category by helping teams create high-converting campaign pages, landing pages, offer pages, and experiment variants.
3. **Full Shopify store builds:** Help merchants generate and launch full Shopify storefronts, including home pages, product pages, collection pages, campaign pages, and brand-aligned sections.
4. **Native Shopify app functionality:** Build commonly used Shopify app capabilities directly inside Conqur AI, so merchants do not need to install multiple external apps. These native modules should match the brand's look and feel instead of looking like generic third-party widgets.

## High-Level GTM Strategy

The near-term go-to-market strategy should start with direct outreach to Shopify merchants and growth agencies, not broad self-serve alone.

The best early merchant targets are Shopify brands with a clear execution gap, regardless of category. High-fit signals include active paid ads, influencer campaigns, seasonal offers, product drops, bundle promotions, old or inconsistent campaign pages, broken mobile sections, frequent theme changes, and visible reliance on freelancers, agencies, or manual theme edits for Shopify changes.

The initial offer should be concierge-assisted and concrete: ask merchants to send three Shopify changes they wanted to make in the last 30 days, then implement one safely on a duplicate theme or preview. This proves the value of faster execution before asking them to fully change their workflow.

Growth and CRO agencies are also an active target channel. The best agency customers are paid growth, CRO, lifecycle, or creative strategy agencies that work with Shopify brands but do not have enough in-house Shopify development capacity. Wherever they currently depend on freelancers, outsourced developers, client-side developers, or slow agency partners for Shopify execution, Conqur AI can become the faster execution layer.

For agencies, the wedge is not replacing their strategy or client relationship. The wedge is helping them ship the Shopify changes their campaigns already need: campaign sections, landing-page variants, offer banners, PDP improvements, bundle sections, mobile fixes, and brand-matched storefront updates. Over time, this channel will need multi-store support, client workspaces, preview links, roles, edit history, rollback, and safe publishing controls.

## Positioning

Conqur AI is not just a page builder and not just a developer tool. The core positioning is:

**Describe the Shopify change you want. Conqur AI turns it into a safe, brand-matched storefront update you can preview, version, and publish.**

The long-term vision is to become the AI execution layer for Shopify: edits, campaign pages, full storefronts, and native commerce functionality, all built around the merchant's brand and conversion goals.
