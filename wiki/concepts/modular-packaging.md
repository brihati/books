  - The chunk discusses Amplitude's pricing strategy and how to create effective price packaging. - [[books/price-to-scale|Price to Scale]] / `chapter-05-step-2` / `chapter-05-step-2-chunk-005`
  - Discussion of modular packaging as a flexible pricing approach that allows tailored offerings to different customer segments. The approach involves mapping product features to use cases, ensuring features are MECE, and estimating value perception across customer segments to determine which modules should be bundled in base packages versus offered as add-ons.
  - Modular packaging enables capturing greater revenue than standardized packages
  - Prevents both shelfware (unused capabilities) and missing value delivery
  - Features must be MECE (Mutually Exclusive Completely Exhaustive) to avoid duplication across modules
- [[books/price-to-scale|Price to Scale]] / `chapter-05-step-2` / `chapter-05-step-2-chunk-006`
  - The chunk covers modular packaging for SaaS products and includes a case study of Medallia's use of marketecture to package hidden product differentiators as distinct modules. It also discusses decision-making criteria for whether to introduce features as add-ons versus incorporating them into main packages.
  - Use case names in packaging need not be final module names
  - Packaging hypothesis should be tested against customer segments to determine package-segment fit
  - Hidden capabilities in enterprise software can be creatively packaged as differentiators
