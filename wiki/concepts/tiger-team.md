  - Jan Pasternak shares his approach to software pricing and lessons from his work at Citrix. He outlines a three-stage pricing methodology: Data Stage (research and hypothesis testing), Testing Stage (A/B testing with different customer cohorts), and Controlled Launch Stage. Key insights include handling commoditization of core features, premium tier differentiation, and the importance of data simpl
  - For established products facing commoditization, focus on premium elements that larger players do not offer
  - Stretch pricing line-up to accommodate masses with basic functionality while pushing 5-7% into premium offerings
  - Data simplicity matters in pricing - fewer tiers lead to higher conversion rates despite potentially worse fit

  - Case study examining Zoom's pricing complexity challenges and solutions. Between 2014-2020, Zoom's rapid expansion led to over 14 add-ons, 1000+ SKUs, and convoluted pricing structure impacting conversions. Zoom established a Pricing Operations team, conducted Conjoint studies to optimize packaging, consolidated add-ons into main packages, and introduced the Zoom One bundle. Success required data-
  - Zoom's rapid growth and speed-to-market approach created pricing complexity through excessive add-ons and SKUs
  - Product overload with 14+ add-ons exceeded 1000 SKUs, negatively impacting customer experience and conversions
  - Convoluted pricing structure made total cost of ownership unclear for customers
