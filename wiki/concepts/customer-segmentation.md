  - The conclusion chapter provides resources, contact information, and a comprehensive cheatsheet of the Mom Test methodology including key skills, the core three rules, methods for getting back on track, and five common mistakes to avoid when conducting customer conversations.
  - Startups are more craft than science; learning by doing is essential
  - The Mom Test consists of three core rules: talk about their life instead of your idea, ask about specifics in the past instead of generics or opinions about the future, and talk less and listen more
  - Key skills include asking good questions, avoiding bad data, keeping conversations casual, pushing for commitment, framing meetings, customer segmentation, and taking notes
