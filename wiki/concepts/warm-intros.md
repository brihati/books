- [[books/the-mom-test|The Mom Test]] / `chapter-08-finding-conversations` / `chapter-08-finding-conversations-chunk-003`
  - Guide to framing meeting requests and conducting in-person customer conversations. Covers the five-element framework for requesting meetings (Vision/Framing/Weakness/Pedestal/Ask), how to control the conversation flow, and why in-person meetings are superior to phone calls or Skype.
  - If you don't know why you're there, the meeting becomes a sales meeting by default, which is bad
  - Bad framing includes 'Can I interview you', 'Can I get your opinion', or 'Do you have time for a quick coffee' without context
  - The five-element framing format: Vision, Framing, Weakness, Pedestal, Ask
