- [[books/price-to-scale|Price to Scale]] / `chapter-11-pricing-example` / `chapter-11-pricing-example-chunk-005`
  - This chunk presents the finalized pricing and packaging model for a customer support product with two tiers (Pro and Elite) based on WTP survey feedback. It details the pricing structure using a 3-part tariff with volume discounts and overage pricing, along with operational decisions for go-to-market implementation.
  - Mid-market customers prioritize support cost reduction and are more price sensitive; enterprise customers prioritize customer satisfaction and agility
  - Mid-market customers show highest WTP at 30 cents per interaction (85% willing); enterprise at 50 cents per interaction (55% willing)
  - 3-part tariff pricing model selected for highest revenue uplift potential
