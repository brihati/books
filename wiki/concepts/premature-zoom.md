- [[books/the-mom-test|The Mom Test]] / `chapter-06-keeping-it-casual` / `chapter-06-keeping-it-casual-chunk-001`
  - The chapter argues against formal, scheduled customer meetings in favor of casual, lightweight conversations. Premature formality creates bias and inefficiency; instead, founders should strip away ceremony and conduct quick chats that feel natural, allowing them to learn about customer problems without the overhead of formal meetings.
  - Zooming prematurely and introducing your idea too early creates biases and can get you stuck on a local maximum
  - The 3-meeting structure (problem, solution, sales) avoids premature bias but is difficult and time-intensive in practice
  - Keeping conversations casual and informal is more effective than formal scheduled meetings
