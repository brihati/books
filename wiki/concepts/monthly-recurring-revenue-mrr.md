- [[books/price-to-scale|Price to Scale]] / `chapter-16-interviews-case-studies-with-top-pricing-leaders` / `chapter-16-interviews-case-studies-with-top-pricing-leaders-chunk-033`
  - Pushpay implemented a proactive pricing strategy to address competitive pressures and customer churn by identifying at-risk customers through churn prediction metrics, offering personalized price reductions (10-30%), improving communication, and iteratively monitoring results. The strategy successfully reduced churn by 20%, improved customer satisfaction, and increased overall revenue by 10%.
  - Churn prediction based on five key metrics: usage frequency, recent usage trends, customer success tickets, price history, and product mix
  - Personalized price reductions (10-30%) combined with requests for longer contract commitments
  - Omnichannel communication strategy using email, in-product messaging, and phone outreach
