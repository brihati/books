And here is a summary of the output of the WTP survey (refer our
discussion on cumulative survey tables in the prior chapter):
Mid-market-Enterprise
-

Based on this feedback we can surmise that the mid-market
customers a) care more about the cost of providing support (often
because of high cost phone channel usage and personnel required)
and that customer satisfaction is important but a second priority b)
they are more price sensitive than enterprise on price point, with the
highest frequency WTP (85% willing to spend) occurring at 30cents
per interaction. Enterprise customers care more about customer
satisfaction and NPS scores, as well as an ability to be agile to
customer needs. Their highest frequency WTP (55% willing to spend)
occurs at 50 cents per interaction.
