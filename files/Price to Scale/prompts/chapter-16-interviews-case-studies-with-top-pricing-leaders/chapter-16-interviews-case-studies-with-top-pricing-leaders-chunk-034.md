structure aimed at accommodating everyone from individual

Dynamics of the 2019 Pricing Tiers
1. Free Tier: The free tier was designed to entice individual
developers and small teams by providing just enough func-
tionality to get started, like 2,000 CI pipeline minutes and
basic project management tools. It offered a glimpse into
GitLab’s capabilities without full commitment.
2. Bronze Tier: Priced at only $4, the Bronze tier served as a
stepping stone and included features like next business day
support and code quality analysis. Yet, it created a comfort
zone that didn't encourage moving to higher tiers. It was too
close to free in price yet too far from Silver in value, offering
little incentive for small businesses to upgrade despite
increased feature access.
