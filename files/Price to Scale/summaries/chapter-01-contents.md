## 5. Definitions and Terms
- **5-Step Pricing Transformation Framework**: Core methodology consisting of five sequential steps for pricing strategy
- **Segmentation**: Market/customer division step
- **Positioning & Packaging**: Brand and product bundling phase
- **Pricing Metric**: Decision on what unit/dimension to charge for
- **Rate Setting**: Establishing price points
- **Pricing Operations**: Execution and management including Deal Desk functions
- **Deal Desk**: Specialized team handling custom deal pricing
- **Usage-Based Pricing**: Charging based on consumption metrics
- **Consumption Pricing**: Customer pays based on usage volume
- **Events-Based Pricing Model**: Charging per discrete events (e.g., Mixpanel example)
- **MTU-Based Pricing**: Monthly Tracked Users—an alternative metric
- **Feature-Based Pricing**: Charging based on feature access/tier
- **Perpetual Licensing Model**: One-time purchase with ongoing use rights
- **Subscription Pricing**: Recurring periodic charges (monthly/annual)
- **Total Cost of Ownership**: Customer-centric pricing perspective (Coralogix approach)

## 8. Actionable Lessons
