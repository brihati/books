# Launch the Invasion

## Detailed Chapter Summary

This chapter brings distribution and pricing into the beachhead strategy. Moore argues that distribution is the vehicle for entering the mainstream market and pricing is the fuel that motivates the channel. During the chasm period, the priority is to establish a channel that pragmatist customers will trust, even above short-term revenue optimization. Pricing should support that channel strategy and reinforce the market position.

## Key Points

- Distribution and pricing are the two marketing decisions that directly touch mainstream customers.
- The number-one objective during the chasm period is establishing the right channel into the mainstream market.
- Pricing should motivate the channel and support positioning, not merely satisfy internal revenue targets.
- Different routes to market imply different pricing and sales economics.

## Important Examples And Stories

- The chapter compares direct sales, web self-service, sales-assisted motions, and channel models as different routes to market.
- Pricing is treated as a channel-design decision as much as a customer-value decision.
