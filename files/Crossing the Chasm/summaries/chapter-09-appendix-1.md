## Detailed Chapter Summary

The appendix material places Crossing the Chasm in Moore’s broader high-tech market-development model. It connects the beachhead strategy to later stages such as the bowling alley, tornado, and main street, and also introduces later thinking about digital consumer adoption. The appendix is useful as a bridge from chasm crossing to what comes after initial mainstream traction.

## Key Points

- The chasm is one stage in a broader market-development sequence.
- The bowling alley expands from one niche to adjacent niches.
- The tornado describes rapid mainstream acceleration once a category standard begins to form.
- Later adoption models adapt the framework for consumer and digital contexts.
