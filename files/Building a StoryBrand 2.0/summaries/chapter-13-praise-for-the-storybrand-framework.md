## Key Points

- PRAISE FOR THE STORYBRAND FRAMEWORK "Before StoryBrand, customers weren't seeing themselves in our offering.
- Sadly, our message, though, was all about us.
- We were talking about our strengths rather than the customer's needs.
- Also, our marketing material was too complex and hard to understand.
- Before StoryBrand, our marketing efforts were a jumbled mess.
- I attended a StoryBrand Marketing Workshop and learned we were talking about the wrong things in our marketing copy.
- We used the framework to simplify our message down to a single tagline.
- We started focusing more on the meaningful work our customers were doing and showing how they could make an even bigger impact around the world just by choosing office products that also make an impact.
