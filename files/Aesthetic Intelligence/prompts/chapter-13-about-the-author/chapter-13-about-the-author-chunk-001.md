About the Author
Throughout her career PAULINE BROWN has acquired, built, and led the
world’s most influential brands. Pauline formerly served as the chairman of
the world’s leading luxury goods company, LVMH Moët Hennessy–Louis
Vuitton North America, where she provided regional leadership for seventy
brands in five sectors, including fashion and leather goods, watches and
jewelry, perfume and cosmetics, wine and spirits, and selective retailing.
In 2016, she joined the faculty of the Harvard Business School, where
she introduced a groundbreaking course called the Business of Aesthetics,
which she taught through 2017. Today, Pauline advises numerous corporate
clients on the power of aesthetic intelligence to transform businesses. She
also hosts a weekly lifestyle show on SiriusXM called Tastemakers,
interviewing a wide array of trendsetters, innovators, and influencers.
Earlier in her career, Pauline held senior executive positions in the global
beauty industry, including head of corporate strategy and new business
development at the Estée Lauder Companies, where she helped to expand
the company’s portfolio from eight to twenty-five distinct beauty brands.
She began her career as a strategy consultant at Bain & Co.
In addition to her operational experience in the luxury goods sector,
Pauline also boasts a successful track record as an investor in luxury brands.
Between her roles at Estée Lauder and LVMH, she was a managing director
at the private equity firm The Carlyle Group, where she focused on deals in
the consumer and retail industries, and served on the boards of portfolio
companies, including the European sportswear brand Moncler S.p.A. and
the American beauty brand Philosophy, Inc.
Pauline currently serves as an independent director of several boards,
including the publicly traded steakhouse chain Del Frisco’s Restaurant
Group. In the nonprofit arena, she sits on the board of overseers of the
Aspen Institute’s Henry Crown Fellowship Program and the board of
governors of the Parsons School of Design. She is a former board member
of the National Retail Federation, Cosmetic Executive Women, and Fashion
Group International. Since 2008, she has been a Henry Crown Fellow of the
Aspen Institute, a program that seeks to develop the next generation of
community-spirited leaders.
Pauline received an MBA from the Wharton School at the University of
Pennsylvania and a BA from Dartmouth College. She is a member of
Wharton’s Alumni Executive Board.
She lives in New York and has two teenage children.
