Here's where we're going: We've seen that compact ideas are stickier, but that compact ideas alone aren't valuable —only ideas with profound compactness are valuable. So, to make a profound idea compact you've got to pack a lot of meaning into a little bit of messaging. And how do you do that? You use flags. You tap the existing memory terrain of your audience. You use what's already there.
