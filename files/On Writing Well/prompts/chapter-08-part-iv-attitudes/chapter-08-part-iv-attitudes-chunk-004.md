Probably the biggest fear for nonfiction writers is the fear of not being able to bring off their assignment. With fiction it's a different situation. Because authors of fiction are writing about a world of their own invention, often in an allusive style that they have also invented (Thomas Pynchon, Don DeLillo), we have no right to tell them, "That's wrong."
