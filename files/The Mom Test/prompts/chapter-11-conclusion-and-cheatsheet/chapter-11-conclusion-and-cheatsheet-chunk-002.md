Results of a good meeting
•
Facts — concrete, speciﬁc facts about what they do and why they do it (as
opposed to the bad data of compliments, ﬂuff, and opinions)
•
Commitment — They are showing they’re serious by giving up something
they value such as time, reputation, or money.
•
Advancement — They are moving to the next step of your real-world
funnel and getting closer to a sale.
