# Chapter 04: Contents

## Detailed Chapter Summary

This chapter presents the table of contents for a comprehensive pricing strategy and tactics textbook organized into 12 chapters spanning pages xiii–317. The book progresses from foundational pricing concepts through advanced applications and organizational implementation.

The chapters are structured thematically around core pricing functions:

**Foundational Concepts** begin with economic value and price communication strategies, establishing the theoretical and communicative basis for pricing decisions.

**Structural and Tactical Elements** cover price structure design, pricing policy formulation, price level setting, and price competition dynamics—the mechanics of how prices are determined and communicated to markets.

**Analytical Capabilities** include measurement of price sensitivity and financial analysis of costs and profits, providing the quantitative tools necessary for informed pricing decisions.

**Advanced Applications** address specialized strategies adapted to common business challenges and customer segmentation tactics for pricing differently across segments.

**Organizational Implementation** focuses on building strategic pricing capability through talent, processes, and data assembly to create competitive advantage.

**Governance** concludes with ethics and legal constraints that bound pricing decisions.

## Key Points

- Book contains 12 sequential chapters covering pricing strategy comprehensively
- Topics progress from foundational concepts to advanced applications and organizational capability
- Pricing strategy is positioned as "the guiding force" coordinating drivers of profitability
- Measurement, financial analysis, and research techniques are integrated throughout
- Ethical and legal constraints are treated as essential governance elements
