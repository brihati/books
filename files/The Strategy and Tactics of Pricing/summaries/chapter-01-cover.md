# 2. Detailed Chapter Summary

*The Strategy and Tactics of Pricing* is a comprehensive reference text designed to teach strategic pricing decisions that proactively manage customer perceptions of value and shift demand curves. Rather than calculating prices merely to cover costs or achieve sales targets, the book emphasizes value-based, strategic pricing as the foundation for business profitability.

This edition represents a significant update to a work that has maintained influence for over three decades. New content includes discussions on behavioral economics and expanded coverage of big data analytics. The book also features a revised chapter addressing specialized strategies, including foreign exchange risks and transfer pricing considerations for profit centers. Most substantially, the chapter on implementing strategic pricing capability in organizations has been completely rewritten to reflect contemporary organizational challenges.

The text is structured to help readers apply classic pricing principles to current challenges in the digital economy and evolving buyer decision-making patterns. It includes in-chapter textboxes with walk-through examples of current pricing challenges, chapter summaries, and visual aids to support both theoretical understanding and practical application.
