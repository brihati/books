# Chapter 09: List of Exhibits

## Detailed Chapter Summary

This chapter provides a comprehensive reference catalog of 92 exhibits distributed across chapters 1-12 of the book. The exhibits serve as visual and analytical tools supporting the strategic pricing concepts and methodologies discussed throughout the text. They encompass both conceptual frameworks and practical analytical instruments designed to guide pricing decisions across various business contexts.

The exhibits are organized thematically around major pricing topics, including value cascade management, economic value estimation, customer segmentation strategies, breakeven analysis, price sensitivity measurement, and pricing organization structures. The collection demonstrates the breadth of pricing applications, from industrial buyer analysis to consumer digital products, and from airline pricing to utility rate structures.

- **Scope**: 92 exhibits referenced across 12 chapters covering strategic pricing concepts and analytical tools

## Definitions and Terms

- **Economic Value Estimation (EVE®) Model**: Proprietary framework for calculating customer value
- **Value Cascade**: Methodology for managing value across customer segments
- **Value-Based Pricing**: Pricing approach anchored to customer perceived value
- **Willingness-to-Pay**: Customer's maximum acceptable price
- **Segmented Price Structure**: Differentiated pricing across customer groups
- **Subscription Pricing**: Recurring revenue pricing model
- **Pricing Metrics**: Quantitative measures for pricing decisions
- **Price Negotiation**: Tactical pricing discussion framework
- **Breakeven Sales Curve**: Analysis of volume required at different price points
- **Price Elasticity of Demand**: Sensitivity of demand to price changes
- **Transfer Pricing**: Internal pricing between business units
- **Price Band Analysis**: Range analysis for acceptable pricing
- **Price Waterfall Analysis**: Decomposition of price components and discounts
- **Customer Profitability Map**: Visual representation of customer value distribution
- **Decision Rights**: Authority structure for pricing decisions
- **Pricing Analytics**: Quantitative analysis supporting pricing strategy
