The breakeven for- mula is a simple way to discover at what point the change in sales becomes large enough to make a price reduction proﬁ t- able, or a price increase unproﬁ table. Exhibit 9B-1 illustrates the break- even problem. At the initial price P, a company can sell the quantity Q.
